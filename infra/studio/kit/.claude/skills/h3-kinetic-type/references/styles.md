# Style presets

English summaries of MiniMax's style references (`h3-visual-design/references/style-*.md`, `td-cv-tracking.md`,
`handdrawn-live-action.md`, the `mv` workflow's route tables and `cool-music-video`'s collage guide). Each preset is a
starting point for the song's STYLE LOCK header, not a template: pick one, then write its palette, type and motion as
visible properties. Their 15-second state arcs are for one 13–15 s clip; for shorter shots keep the first, the strongest and
the last state. Their music profiles describe the sound a preset expects; with the real song locked, use them only to judge
fit.

Every preset shares MiniMax's limits: glitches are short, countable and restored; text is complete before it deforms; no fake
HUD, codes, coordinates, issue numbers, prices or logos; nothing covers eyes or mouth.

## Fresh cute (hand-drawn variety opening)

- **Palette**: chalk white `#FFF8E9` plus one deep ink (cocoa `#382822`, forest `#173F36` or cobalt `#1649B8`); bright pairs
  that rotate per state (sky blue/grass green/signal yellow; wood/apricot/watermelon; cobalt/leaf/coral). 3–4 colors visible at
  a time; real footage keeps its own colors, the brights go on outlines, drawn scenery and wipes.
- **Type**: Display A quirky chunky display (uneven heavy strokes, round and sharp corners, white or bright face, a 2–4%
  hand-drawn outline and a 3–7% flat one-direction extrusion); Display B chalk, crayon or marker; Utility a calm humanist or
  rounded sans or neat handwriting.
- **Materials**: live-action cutouts, chalk and crayon lines with 6–10 fps redraw jitter, paper-photo collage, flat
  illustration.
- **Motion**: line grows from a real edge (0.25–0.7 s); freeze-frame cast (0.45–0.9 s) with a spot-color outline; photo page
  swap; live-to-drawn and back; doodle morph through 2–4 key shapes; color-shape wipe from a shape seen earlier; title assembly
  holding 1.0–1.4 s. Rebound only when the content jumps or collides, never on every word.
- **Arc**: photo cold open → freeze-frame cast → drawn interaction → collage/color change → title resolve.
- **Sound fit**: light 4/4, 100–118 BPM, mallets, muted claps.
- **Avoid**: one pastel rounded font everywhere, rainbow everything, decorations with no source in the picture.

## Tech particle

- **Palette**: near-black `#05090F` or navy `#071726`, cold white `#EAF6FF` text, one energy color (cyan `#22D3EE`, ice blue
  `#4CA6FF` or acid green `#9CFF57`); warning orange-red `#FF5C45` under 8% and only when it means something. Bright footage is
  not crushed into a blue-black filter.
- **Type**: Display A wide geometric or heavy condensed grotesk, solid; Display B modular stencil or line-frame letters for
  outlines, repetition fields and portals; Utility mono or neutral, real labels only. Glow radius under 8% of letter height.
- **Motion**: 60–120 particles leave a real highlight or edge, travel a visible path, gather into the exact word in
  0.45–0.8 s, stop and become solid for ≥0.8 s, one outline pulse, then return along the same path. Scan reveal: a line 4% of
  letter height wipes left to right in 0.35 s. Data slice: three strips, the middle one offset ≤4% of a letter width for 2–3
  frames, once.
- **Arc**: stage → orbit with particle gathering → hero hold → real detail → one scan response → particles home.
- **Sound fit**: precise 4/4 electronics, 110–128 BPM, ticks and one clean impact.
- **Avoid**: random HUD, pseudo-code, loading bars, permanent glitch, particles from nowhere, full-frame bloom.

## Cool art (fused refraction poster)

- **Palette**: mist white to light silver (`#ECEDEE`–`#F7F7F5`), graphite `#202124`, signal red-orange (`#EB3C24`–`#FF4A16`,
  8–30% of the added design), a micro acid green `#C7FF2E` (≤5%, only on one real piece of information); one 0.10–0.25 s
  inversion in the whole piece.
- **Type**: one fused family. Display A wide, heavy modular geometric with 6–12% horizontal slots; Display B the same
  neo-grotesk family in regular/bold/condensed/italic, set on arcs and transparent tapes; Utility semi-mono. No default
  serif or brush.
- **Materials**: clear crumpled plastic, frosted glass, chrome reflection, one red-orange line that persists through every
  state.
- **Motion**: refraction shifts what's behind by 1–3%; fold lines become a 4×4 or 6×4 grid; letters leave the baseline into a
  180–300° arc; an information tape folds into a readable 3D type block that turns 90–180°; one face hard-cuts into a dense
  black-and-orange event poster; a scan line and a single 2–4 frame pixel stretch; the line returns as the final baseline.
- **Arc**: refractive reveal → type morph around the subject → type block/event poster → dark scan fracture → fused poster.
- **Space**: type lives on at least two planes sharing a vanishing point (floor to wall, glass to foreground board).
- **Sound fit**: fused editorial electronics, 112–124 BPM, foil flex, glass ticks, one block hit, one scan zap.
- **Avoid**: four separate effect stacks, fake tickers or data, continuous glitch, speed lines, full-frame strobe.

## Dark-pop glitch (underground zine, cyber-grunge)

- **Palette**: deep black, charcoal, saturated blood red, dirty white (Blood Zine: `#050607`, `#F4F1EA`, `#E82018`,
  `#77706B`). Red only on the hero, cracks, registration offsets and the final cover accent.
- **Type**: Display A heavy condensed sans or modular bold black (the title or hook); Display B a different editorial phrase,
  vertical or in a side column; Utility a narrow semi-mono for real short labels. Marks: crop marks, halftone, registration
  crosses, ink, film scratches.
- **Materials**: 16 mm grain, photocopy halftone, scan misregistration, ink bleed, exposure burn.
- **Motion**: hard cuts, jump cuts and single-frame inserts only. One-frame negative flash; 2–4 frame scan tear; red/black
  registration offset once; 2–4 frame halftone macro inserts; photocopy rip as a wipe; 0.10–0.25 s exposure burn on an edge;
  particles that spray and are sucked back. Each restores the subject and the exact spelling at once.
- **Arc**: still pressure → scan tear/word shock → zine collage attack → red impact on the strongest beat → magazine cover
  freeze held 1.0–1.4 s.
- **Sparse text**: with only one or two words, don't stack the same word large in every state; density comes from counters,
  crops, scan slits and non-readable marks.
- **Sound fit**: 78–96 BPM half-time or 120–144 trap feel, distorted 808, tape hiss.
- **Avoid**: rainbow neon, RGB glitch beds, monsters/skulls, random letters, long strobes, fades.

## Poster collage (big-type print)

- **Palette**: paper white (`#F2EBDD` or `#F7F5EF`), ink black `#101010`, one spot color: brick red `#D94A3A`, ultramarine
  `#2457D6` or acid yellow `#E7C83A`. Colorful footage keeps its colors; the type layer stays black, paper and one spot.
- **Type**: Display A ultra-bold black, condensed grotesk or heavy Song/serif; Display B a modern serif, heavy brush or line
  frame letters; Utility a small neutral sans or neat hand. Two textures from: paper fiber, uneven ink, halftone,
  registration offset, assigned to different states.
- **Motion**: a paper strip 20–60% of the frame slides in from a stated side and stops with the word printed on it, readable
  ≥0.8 s, leaves the way it came or is pinned by the subject; a torn seam runs across a complete hero with 2–4% offset on each
  side; a 3–6 px spot-color registration shift for 2–3 frames; halftone grows from one corner of the word and shrinks back.
- **Retro cut-paper variant**: 1–2 muted spot colors, the subject as a complete paper cutout, 3–5 torn pieces with entry →
  turn or slide → snap → hold → exit, one info card; clean stage → paper assembly → type/subject exchange → detail card →
  dense final board.
- **Arc**: full-frame printed hero, a multi-panel state, one glyph or panel becoming the transition.
- **Sound fit**: tactile 4/4, 90–108 BPM, dry break, paper and print clicks.
- **Avoid**: fake magazines, issue numbers, authors, prices; confetti from nowhere; texture over the whole frame.

## TD / CV tracking ("the world as a computer sees it")

Not a type style but an overlay look, and the most rule-bound preset:

- 12–20 hollow axis-aligned rectangles at once, 1–2 px stroke, no fill, in four size tiers (large 15–25% of frame width,
  medium 8–14%, small 4–7%, micro 1–3%; 2–4/3–5/3–5/4–6 of each); each tracks a local fragment, none encloses the whole
  subject or head (the largest ≤¼ of the subject's area, the smallest ≤1/32 of the largest); no two share a center.
- Thin straight lines link box corners and centers into a flexing network.
- Small monospace 4-digit IDs or [x, y] labels by each box that change every frame. These are the only non-lyric characters
  any preset allows, and they are deliberately unreadable noise.
- A true photographic negative only inside a few small or micro boxes, switching on accents and rotating to different boxes
  on successive beats; never global black-and-white, strobe or tint.
- 30–50% of boxes jump or resize on each downbeat.
- Underlying footage: low saturation, teal shadows, warm highlights, strong 35 mm grain, single key or back light, shallow
  focus, light vignette; the overlay gets none of the grading.
- MiniMax generates an instrumental track for it; for us the song is locked and the boxes follow its beats. Lyric words, if
  any, should be code-drawn on top: the box layer already spends H3's attention on dense small marks.

## Hand-drawn live action

Real lived-in spaces filmed handheld on a phone (shake, lag, focus hunting, exposure shifts, compression) with one rough 2D
creature or object in crayon, chalk, colored pencil or pastel that keeps redrawing slightly, moves through the whole space,
touches real surfaces (contact, occlusion, reflection, perspective) and morphs continuously with visible in-between shapes.
The filmer's real hand interacts at least twice (action → reaction → the camera follows). 4–5 time segments of one action
each; real ambience, no music, no captions or logos. For a lyric video: either draw the lyric words in code on top, or ask
for them hand-lettered in the same crayon medium as part of the drawn world (one short word, held, then scribbled away);
the latter is untested.

## MiniMax's MV route families (lyric videos)

From the `mv` workflow's type routes, by style family. Layer A is the hero word, B its echo, C the action layer.

| Family | Hero (Layer A) | Echo (Layer B) | Action (Layer C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut-paper collage | Extra-condensed heavy grotesk or stencil, acid wide/narrow contrast | Photocopy, halftone, overprint, torn paper, screen print | Letters through seams, poster edges and the performer's moves |
| Cute animation | Rounded, handwritten, bubble or jelly | Crayon or marker outline, soft shadow, sticker edge | Stars, flowers, small comic lines and letters along the motion |
| Stage effects | LED screen, floor screen, projection or a standing 3D title | Wireframe, laser, particles, mirror or projection echo | Letters along light beams, dance steps and the lens path |
| Y2K retro | Acid, CRT/pixel, chrome or bubblegum display | RGB split, scan lines, liquid plastic, broadcast layer | Cursor, stickers, window fragments, fisheye and flash moves |
| Cinematic narrative | Serif or humanist sans keyword, archive or spatial title | Film grain, reflection, flare, handwriting or environment echo | Letters carried by wind, glass, road, photographs, a gaze |

Collage and Y2K usually put type in 60–100% of the cuts of a 15 s stretch; cute and stage 40–80%; cinematic 2–4 quiet events
(3–6 at the hook). Still one main word and one reading focus at a time.

## Retro trend collage (`cool-music-video`)

Ink black `#111111`, paper white `#F1EBDD`, warning red `#C51F2D` (one color may be swapped for a reference's, four colors
at most); heavy grain, photocopy, halftone, registration offsets, torn edges, tape. Ultra-black condensed grotesk ALL CAPS
heroes (30–150% of the frame), with one secondary family at most (narrow sans, mono or a marker accent). Per shot one meaning
word, built as Layer A hero base, Layer B print plate, Layer C action/foreground. Methods bound to sounds: photocopy/halftone
on a snare, misregistration on a hi-hat roll, torn-paper occlusion cuts on a sung keyword, uneven panels on an 808, a
text-only black frame for 0.2–0.5 s at a pause. At least two design-led shots where the performer stands still and the
layout carries the hit. On-screen text there is English-only by default, 3–6 short phrases drawn from the lyric for 15 s.
