<reference_grammar>
Take: editorial paper, generous margins, file syntax, restrained camera moves.
Take: 2-3 second ideas and one dominant action per beat.
Never take another creator's copy, logos, characters or exact layouts.
</reference_grammar>

<palette>
PAPER         #F3F0E8   main canvas and cards
INK           #171714   primary type and deep backgrounds
CLAUDE CORAL  #D97757   active state and key transition
SOFT GRAY     #B8B2A8   secondary labels and inactive rules
REVIEW BLUE   #2F6BFF   annotations only, maximum 10% of a frame

Coral marks decisions, not decoration. Meet AA contrast for essential text.
</palette>

<typography>
Display: Source Serif 4 Semibold — editorial statements.
UI: Inter Regular / Semibold — labels, captions and scores.
Code: IBM Plex Mono Medium — filenames, commands and timestamps.
Maximum three sizes per frame. Uppercase UI tracking: 0.08em.
No line longer than 42 characters in vertical format.
</typography>

<layout>
12-column grid for 16:9. 6-column grid for 9:16. Safe area: 6%.
Hero content uses 60-70% of the frame; the rest is breathing room.
Align type, cards and motion paths to shared axes.
Never pin critical labels to extreme corners.
</layout>

<shape_language>
Paper sheets: 18px radius, 1px warm-gray border.
Technical nodes: circles and capsules with flat color.
Connectors: 2px with rounded caps.
Claude starburst appears once as a signature, never as wallpaper.
</shape_language>

<texture_and_depth>
Paper grain: 2-3% opacity, locked to screen space.
One soft directional shadow. No glow.
Depth comes from scale, occlusion, focus and parallax — not glass panels.
</texture_and_depth>

<motion_personality>
Precise, calm, authored. UI overshoot: 2-4%. Type: critically damped.
Large paper objects feel heavy. One playful mascot reaction only.
</motion_personality>

<anti_style>
No neon cyberpunk. No liquid blobs. No random particles. No permanent HUD.
No centered gradient title after the opening parody. No AI magic sparkles.
</anti_style>
