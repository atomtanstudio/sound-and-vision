### H3 release constraint

The current official agreement defines excluded territories as the EU, UK,
Republic of Korea and US (I.5); its grant is limited to the applicable territory
(II), and V.4 also addresses use/display of works and outputs outside that territory.
It invites a separate license request for deployment in excluded territories.
The standard agreement therefore does not support a blanket promise of global
unrestricted distribution or operation. Establish the intended deployment's actual
permission, or review a replaceable video adapter, before that integration/release
decision. No account permission or license has been requested on the user's behalf.

Distribution conditions include supplying the agreement, modification notices,
applicable use restrictions and a NOTICE for non-hosted distribution. Read the
exact artifact's complete agreement before shipping; this matrix is not a substitute.
Source: [official H3 agreement](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Installed YuE2 backend

YuE2 source and documentation are pinned at `92a73cc7652fcc1f937855e4b765e0a0edd7ff2e`; their Apache-2.0 license and original notices are retained in `docs/upstream/yue2/`. Generator and listening-decoder weights retain CC BY-NC4.0 and are downloaded into operator-controlled model storage, not included in source or build output. Model cards and original weight manifests are kept in the documentation snapshot. Exact revisions are in `deploy/source.lock.json`.

The City Lights backend baseline uses the upstream original example lyrics from `examples/song.json`. Homeward Light uses original lyrics authored for the integration check. Both names identify actual generated verification media, distinct from the earlier synthetic instrumental fixtures.
